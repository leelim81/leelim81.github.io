Approach · 09 What has to be true10 / 12

Three things could break this.
Here is how each one closes.

Risk 01 · Reach

10X Analyst is
a US offering

  • Zora AI is already regional, run from the Asia Pacific CoE. 10X Analyst is listed by Deloitte US on AWS Marketplace.
  • Answer: confirm in week one that the FS stack can be sold into SEA. Zora plus Claude covers the rest.
Risk 02 · Independence

Audit clients cannot
pay contingent fees

  • If most of the twelve are Deloitte-audited, the outcome-priced model narrows sharply.
  • Answer: count the restricted names before pitching; route them to standard fees.
Risk 03 · Delivery

The bench is an ask,
not a resource

  • Five to six live deployments per GP is a staffing commitment, not a sales one.
  • Answer: a named T&T pod committed before engagement two.

None of these depend on a client saying yes. All three sit inside the firm's control.
