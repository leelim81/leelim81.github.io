# leelim81.github.io

## 🎯 AI Job Swiper — live at **[leelim81.github.io/tindercv](https://leelim81.github.io/tindercv/)**

A Tinder-style app for triaging AI / ML job leads in Singapore.

- Swipe **← Pass · ↑ KIV · → Interested**
- **Interested** downloads Lee Lim's CV and marks the role **Applied**
- Decisions are saved in your browser — use **Export CSV** to sync back to Notion
- Self-contained single page: 76 roles sourced from LinkedIn job alerts + live job portals (MyCareersFuture, Indeed, Jobstreet)

👉 **Open the app: https://leelim81.github.io/tindercv/**
